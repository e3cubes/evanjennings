01 / About

Mid-market companies shouldn't have to choose between operational chaos and enterprise budgets.

After building analytics infrastructure at AWS and high-growth startups, I kept seeing the same pattern: mid-market companies drowning in spreadsheets while their competitors automated everything. The enterprise playbook existed — but nobody was translating it for companies with limited budgets and timelines.

So in January 2025, I founded Quantify to bridge that gap. We bring enterprise-grade Analytics, Automation, and AI to commercial ops leaders who need competitive capabilities without the typical cost or complexity.

In under a year: zero to nearly 20 clients and a team of 4.
